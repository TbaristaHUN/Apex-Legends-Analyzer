HOW DPS IS CALCULATED

The calculator estimates theoretical damage per second using the selected hit-zone damage and the weapon's fire rate.

DPS = Damage × RPM ÷ 60

This value does not include reload time, recoil, missed shots, attachments, damage falloff or player accuracy.

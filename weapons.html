ABOUT THE WEAPON DATABASE

Weapon statistics are loaded dynamically from the Apex//Analyzer PostgreSQL database. Damage values, fire rate and magazine capacity are used by the custom scoring model to calculate statistical weapon ratings.

The current Meta Score is an experimental analytical value and does not represent an official Electronic Arts or Respawn Entertainment ranking.

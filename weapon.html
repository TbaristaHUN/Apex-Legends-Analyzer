METHODOLOGY

HOW THE ANALYZER WORKS

META SCORE

The Meta Score is a custom statistical rating developed for Apex//Analyzer. The current model evaluates body damage, fire rate and Level III magazine capacity.

DPS CALCULATION

Theoretical DPS is calculated from body damage and rounds per minute using the following formula:

DPS = Body Damage × RPM ÷ 60

TIER CLASSIFICATION

Weapons are grouped according to their Analyzer Score:

  • S Tier: 9–10 points
  • A Tier: 7–8 points
  • B Tier: 5–6 points
  • C Tier: 0–4 points

MODEL LIMITATIONS

The current model does not yet account for recoil, reload time, projectile speed, attachments, effective range, player accuracy or weapon-class-specific behavior.
